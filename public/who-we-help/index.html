Who we work with.

Different businesses. A shared ambition to grow with stronger leadership, clearer ownership and more capable teams.

Owners and founders

When the business needs to grow beyond the owner’s personal capacity. Work on priorities, delegation, management routines and a leadership team that can carry more.

Family-business leaders

When modernisation needs to respect relationships and legacy. Bring clarity to roles, generational perspectives, operational change and the next stage of growth.

Growing brands and businesses

When rising demand exposes weak handovers, inconsistent service or unclear ownership. Build a stronger organisation around the opportunity.

CEOs and leadership teams

When growth or transformation requires leaders to work together differently. Connect strategic intent with choices, accountability and the realities of operating the business.

Business context.
Not a sector gate.

Aabhishek’s experience spans B2B, B2C, CPG, retail, travel and hospitality. These examples show the kinds of interconnected priorities a coaching conversation can explore—not claimed client results.

B2B & manufacturing

Orders arrive by email and phone, visibility is fragmented and customers rely on people to find answers. The conversation connects commercial relationships, process ownership, team capacity and appropriate digital support.

Retail & consumer goods

Online and in-store operations behave like separate businesses. Inventory, collection, returns and supplier relationships need a shared view—and leadership that owns the whole customer experience.

D2C & ecommerce

Traffic grows but conversion or margins do not. Acquisition costs, promotions, fulfilment and disconnected reporting compete for attention. Coaching keeps the commercial choices connected.

Travel & hospitality

Service depends on coordination across people, locations and channels. Expansion asks for consistent standards, effective handovers and leaders who can support a strong guest experience.

Startups & scale-ups

The product has traction, but each new customer or channel adds complexity. The priority may be clearer roles, management discipline and systems that can support growth without constant firefighting.
