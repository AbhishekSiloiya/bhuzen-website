Privacy on this staging site.

A clear account of what happens to your information.

Updated 10 October 2026. This public review site is operated by BHUZEN LTD. Visitor form delivery is not yet enabled. This notice describes staging, not a final production policy.

Read the details

Who to contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning information held by Bhuzen, email aabhisheksiloya708@gmail.com. Please do not include confidential business records or other people’s personal information in preview forms.

Forms and your self-audit

Public enquiry, resource and channel-evaluation forms currently validate in your browser but do not send their contents. Controlled owner-authorised delivery checks use synthetic details in an isolated test environment, not an activated public form. No resource delivery or marketing subscription is active.

Self-audit answers, revenue and financial scenarios remain in the current page’s memory; the website does not send or save them to a browser database. “Clear my answers” resets the briefing. Your browser may restore values through its own navigation or autofill features. The self-audit is an illustrative reflection tool, not a financial prediction or an automated decision about you.

Hosting and browsing context

This review site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which records visitor IP addresses for security. GitHub describes its processing in its privacy statement. Public staging is not password-protected: search exclusion is not access control.

The website code does not add analytics, advertising trackers or cookie-based personalisation. Page and asset requests reach the host. Enquiry links may include source page, CTA label, topic, industry, assessment priority and campaign labels; those labels can appear in browser history and hosting logs. Do not put personal or confidential information in link parameters. Names, messages, assessment answers and financial inputs are not placed in enquiry URLs by the website.

Delivery providers and retention

The planned form service is Web3Forms, with enquiries routed to Bhuzen’s Gmail inbox. When enabled, it will receive submitted contact details, relevant business context, the message and limited source/campaign labels—not self-audit financial inputs or answer sets. Its privacy notice describes storage, spam screening and processing across regions. The Bhuzen account’s submission retention setting is three months; this does not delete copies already received in Gmail.

Mailbox retention, provider transfer safeguards and the production processing arrangements require final review before public form activation. We do not claim that this staging notice resolves those launch requirements.

Separate destinations

Google Calendar booking, Google Meet, Aabhishek’s website and the Bhuzen Brief operate separately. Their own privacy notices apply when you visit or use them. A form enquiry does not reserve an appointment. See Google’s privacy policy for its services.

Your choices and rights

You can contact Bhuzen about access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection concerning personal information it holds, and portability where applicable. Rights depend on the circumstances. You may also raise a concern with the Information Commissioner’s Office. The final policy must set out the actual purposes, lawful bases, retention periods and any applicable transfer arrangements before public collection begins.
