Privacy in
this preview.

Clear about what is—and is not—being collected.

This statement describes the local review site. It is not the final policy for the publicly hosted Bhuzen website.

Read the preview details

Forms and assessment answers

Enquiry, resource and channel-evaluation forms validate in your browser but do not send their contents. Assessment answers, annual revenue, estimated cost and optional time inputs stay in the current page’s memory. They are not stored in a browser database or sent to Bhuzen. Use “Clear my answers” to reset the form and briefing. Your browser may restore form values when you return using its navigation controls.

Local requests

The local preview server may log requests for page and asset URLs. Names, email addresses, messages, assessment answers, scores and financial inputs are not placed in those URLs. Enquiry links can carry the originating page, CTA label, selected topic, business context, assessment priority and campaign labels. These URL values may appear in local request logs or browser history. Equivalent hidden form fields prepare this context for future routing but are not sent in this preview. This preview does not include analytics, marketing trackers or cookie-based personalisation.

Links to other services

The booking calendar, Aabhishek’s professional website and The Bhuzen Brief are separate destinations. If you choose to visit them, their own privacy arrangements apply. Completing a booking there is different from testing a local enquiry form.

Before public launch

The final policy must identify the business responsible for personal information, the verified contact details, actual form and email providers, purposes and legal basis, retention, relevant transfers and how people can exercise their rights. Optional marketing consent must remain separate from fulfilment of a resource request.

Those arrangements are not activated or confirmed by this preview. Avoid entering confidential business information or real customer data during testing.
