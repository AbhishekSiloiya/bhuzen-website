Serious ambition.
Clear commitments.

Aabhishek SiloyaFounder, Bhuzen

You should know what you are committing to—and what you can expect in return.

Our promise is to bring focus, honest challenge and a disciplined coaching relationship to the growth of your business. Progress is something we work on together, not a number we promise before we know you.

Discuss your business ambitions

What you can
expect from us.

Commitments that belong in the day-to-day work, not just on this page.

Your business sets the agenda.

We agree the ambitions and priorities with you. Coaching stays connected to your business, your leadership team and the realities of putting decisions into practice.

In practice: shared priorities and a clear reason for the work we focus on.

Honest challenge. Practical focus.

We will question assumptions, raise difficult issues respectfully and explain our reasoning. We will be clear when a question needs specialist expertise beyond the coaching relationship.

In practice: candid conversations that lead to considered decisions and next actions.

Accountability on both sides.

We agree responsibilities and a coaching rhythm. We prepare, follow through on our agreed actions and review progress with you. Where something is not moving, we address the reason rather than simply repeat the plan.

In practice: actions with owners, agreed review points and visible follow-through.

Progress you can examine.

We define meaningful measures together and revisit them regularly. Financial performance may be part of that picture, alongside leadership ownership, operational reliability and the owner’s capacity to lead.

In practice: an agreed starting point and an honest view of what is—and is not—changing.

A relationship you can question.

If the coaching is not meeting the agreed expectations, we want that raised early. We will review the concern together, agree what needs to change and discuss whether continuing is appropriate.

In practice: clear scope, fees, review arrangements and any notice or exit terms agreed in writing before we start.

We bring the coaching.
You bring the commitment.

Neither side can do the other’s part.

Coaching is most useful when the owner and leadership make room for it in how the business actually runs.

  • Be open about the business.

    Bring the relevant information, competing priorities and difficult realities—not only the polished version.

  • Make time for the work.

    Participate in the agreed sessions and involve the people who need to own the changes.

  • Act, then bring back what happened.

    Follow through on agreed actions. If capacity, circumstances or priorities change, say so early and adjust together.

  • Keep ownership where it belongs.

    You retain responsibility for business decisions, resources and implementation. We support your judgment and capability; we do not replace your leadership.

Doing nothing
has a cost too.

The right question is not simply “What does coaching cost?” It is also “What are our current ways of working costing us?” The answer may support coaching, an internal change or another kind of help.

Ambitious about progress.
Honest about outcomes.

Results depend on the starting position, decisions, implementation, resources and circumstances of the business. We do not promise a fixed revenue increase, profit multiple or growth deadline.

What we can commit to is the quality and discipline of our coaching, clear expectations, and a willingness to review the relationship openly.

Specific scope, fees, confidentiality, cancellation arrangements and any agreed remedies belong in the written engagement terms. No automatic refund or financial-return guarantee is offered on this page.
